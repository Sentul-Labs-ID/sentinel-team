Differentiators

Three things SENTINEL does that nothing else does well.

01

Verifiable Carbon Accounting

Cryptographically auditable emission tracking. Every shipment-level calculation is anchored with SHA-256 provenance and Merkle-tree aggregation, producing carbon reports that withstand external ESG audit — not just satisfy internal reporting.

02

Multi-Vector Fraud Detection

Predictive ML detection across the high-impact fraud vectors of Indonesian e-commerce: driver collusion, ghost delivery, GPS spoofing, and COD syndicate behavior. Graph analytics, anomaly detection, and behavioral signals — combined, not in isolation.

03

Cascading Failure Simulation

Graph-based ripple-effect modeling over the hub network. Predicts how a single hub disruption propagates across the system — and recommends pre-emptive load balancing before the cascade begins. Designed for peak events: Lebaran, Harbolnas, 9.9, 11.11.
